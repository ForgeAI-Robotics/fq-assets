G1 head-camera slot geometry (provisional)

G1 O6 0.1.3; G1 O7 0.1.2. Restore a camera pocket in the shared head shell, retaining the original vendor mesh. The 94 x 29 mm pocket is based on the current D435i CAD envelope with nominal 2 mm clearance; it is NOT a measurement of the physical head slot. Four convex collision pieces retain the opening instead of filling it with one convex hull.

The head-mounted D435i gets idealized optical channels through opaque display-CAD glass/internal parts. Only the head instance changes; standalone camera and wrist assets are unchanged. This is a pinhole rendering approximation, not an optical lens or infrared emitter simulation. Camera extrinsics/intrinsics, body mass/inertia, joints and actuators are unchanged. Head contact geometry intentionally changes.

Validation: O6/O7, three waist poses, 825 rays per camera/pose. RGB head obstruction 0/825; native depth head-edge obstruction 5/825 remains visible in the audit. Camera housing obstruction 0/825 after idealized channels. Low-near-clip (0.1 mm) first-person render confirms the RGB view is no longer hidden by opaque CAD. Conservative collision proxies can intersect optical rays but do not render. The SIMPLE camera box is geometrically separated from each head convex piece by >=1.9 mm; mj_geomDistance reports zero for the rear piece, recorded rather than represented as a successful solver-clearance test.

SIMPLE sync is isolated in SIMPLE-head-camera-20261013, with four MJCF variants and URDF updated. Two USD files are updated and reopened for structural validation; live Isaac/PhysX runtime is not tested. Existing running tasks and the old physical robot are untouched.

The prior calibration remains pending independent physical validation, including its 10 mm torso-height bridge discrepancy. Slot dimensions and CAD housing offset remain approximate. No claim of full native-depth edge visibility or physical-camera accuracy is made.

Rebuild from v0.3.3 in an isolated checkout: build_head_slot.py, then open_head_camera_optics.py. Dependencies: trimesh 4.4.9, manifold3d 3.5.4, MuJoCo 3.3.6, NumPy. The build scripts are intended for a fresh parent release, not an already-generated mesh tree.
